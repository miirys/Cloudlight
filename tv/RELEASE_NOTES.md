Cloudlight is a GeForce NOW client for Android TV.

**Streaming**
- Up to 1440p at 120 fps over WebRTC, with HEVC, AV1 and H.264
- Experimental 4K at 60 fps (Settings, Stream): streams over NVIDIA's native protocol, the only kind of session GeForce NOW gives 4K to, and goes back to the normal stream by itself if it can't start
- On Amlogic boxes the decoder draws straight onto the TV's video layer, with no copy through the graphics chip, so a 120 fps stream is not held back by the screen
- A 20 Mbps bitrate floor, and a frame pacing choice in Settings for the standard video output
- Resume sessions started on other devices, at the quality you chose
- Picture in picture when you press Home during a game

**Account**
- Stays signed in between launches; the sign-in renews itself in the background

**Controls**
- Controllers over Bluetooth or USB, including 2.4 GHz dongles the TV doesn't recognise
- A controller works in the menus and in the game straight away, without touching the remote first
- Mouse and keyboard pass straight through to the game
- An on-screen keyboard for typing into games and sign-in pages (stream menu, then Keys)

**Interface**
- Runs the TV at its fastest refresh rate for smooth menus (can be turned off in Settings)
- Spring animations and a monochrome glass look

Install the APK below on your TV. If you had a test build installed, uninstall it once first;
from now on, updates install over each other.
